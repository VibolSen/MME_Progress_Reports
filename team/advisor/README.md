# Advisor Desk · Strategic Alignment & Roadmap Advisory

**Author:** Advisor Specialist  
**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Task:** Team Desk Setup - Advisor  

## 🎯 Role Overview
The **Advisor Desk** aligns the digital platform with executive requirements from the **Ministry of Mines and Energy (MME)**, oversees inter-ministerial coordination, and shapes the long-term delivery roadmap.

## 🧭 Key Focus Areas
1. **Stakeholder Coordination**:
   * Guide inter-ministerial engagement between MME and MEF / Public Administration regarding the Payroll System (Component 1.2.2).
   * Accelerate requirement validation workshops for State Asset Management (1.3) and Budget Planning (1.4).
2. **Three-Department Scope Ingestion**:
   * Oversee the intake and transition of Department 2 and Department 3 from "In Preparation" to active tracking.
   * Ensure uniform governance across all 3 departments.
3. **Executive Presentation Advisory**:
   * Ensure slide narratives clearly articulate the 3 core pillars:
     1. Progress and Pending
     2. Challenges & Mitigations
     3. Expected Completion & Timeline

## 📋 Standard Workflow
1. Assess project data against MME strategic goals.
2. Formulate actionable recommendations and mitigation strategies for pending items.
3. Update slide content in [`app/slidesData.ts`](../../app/slidesData.ts) to reflect executive decisions.
